Privacy Policy — OwnIt DashCam

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version: OwnIt DashCam collects nothing. Everything the app records stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no subscriptions.

1. What the app accesses and why

  • Camera — to record front- and rear-camera video for dashcam use.
  • Microphone — to record audio with your videos. You can mute it at any time in the app.
  • Location (GPS) — to display and optionally burn your speed and coordinates into recordings as an on-screen stamp.
  • Storage — to save your videos and photos to the NoSubDashCam folder on your device.

2. Where your data goes

Nowhere. All videos, photos, GPS coordinates, and speed data are stored only on your device, in folders you control. The app does not transmit, upload, sync, or share any data with us or anyone else. It works fully offline.

3. Data we collect

None. Empire STS does not collect, receive, or store any personal data, usage data, crash data, or media from OwnIt DashCam.

4. Deleting your data

Your recordings are yours. Delete them anytime using the app's auto-clear settings, your file manager, or by uninstalling the app. Auto-clear and loop-recording deletion happen entirely on your device.

5. Third parties

The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud services. No data is shared with any third party because no data leaves your phone.

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
